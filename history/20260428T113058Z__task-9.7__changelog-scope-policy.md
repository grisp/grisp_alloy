## Key Decisions
- Changelog updates are now conditional rather than mandatory: they belong only
  when a task changes behavior, interfaces, outputs, supported workflow, or
  another repository contract that is useful to users or contributors reading
  the changelog.
- Internal planning/history/task-bookkeeping changes should stay out of the
  changelog even when they are committed.
- The workflow/agent guidance should name local signing/pinentry/amend friction
  explicitly as history-file noise, not just rely on generic "session-state"
  wording.
- The previous root/bootstrap audit commit should be amended in place to remove
  its internal-only changelog entry rather than preserving a follow-up changelog
  correction commit.
